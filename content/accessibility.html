Website Accessibility

Centene is committed to providing accessible digital experiences in accordance with federal laws, such as the Americans with Disabilities Act and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, and state laws such as the  Unruh Act of California and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Centene demonstrates that commitment through the adoption of and meaningful conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 levels A and AA.

We welcome your feedback on our digital accessibility efforts. Please contact us at accessibility@centene.com. Emails are generally responded to within 3 business days.

 

Third-Party Websites

Some Centene websites may contain links to web pages owned and operated by third parties. Centene does not guarantee the accessibility of those third-party sites. For more information, view our legal disclaimer.

 

Accessibility Assistance

For callers with hearing disabilities, please dial 711 and follow the prompts.
