"As a leading Medicaid managed care organization, Centene understands that CHCs are often where our members turn when they need care. For so many individuals and families, they're a trusted partner that knows the community, understands the challenges people face and provides them the care and support they need," said Alice Chen, Chief Health Officer, Centene.

"At Centene, we know that improving health outcomes starts with meeting people where they are. CHCs are key partners for us in that work, from supporting healthy pregnancies and improving maternal health outcomes to managing chronic conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure and expanding access to behavioral health services. That's why we invest in CHCs: they help people get the right care at the right time and transform the health of the communities we serve."

Alice Hm Chen, Chief Health Officer, Centene

"What makes CHCs unique, and an essential part of the healthcare system, is that they are governed by patients for patients. Fifty-one percent of CHC board members are patients, and these patients receive the same wraparound care unique to their communities from their centers,” said Kyu Rhee, MD, MPP, CEO and President of NACHC. “At only 1% of the total U.S. healthcare spend, CHCs provide comprehensive whole-person care that extends beyond the exam room while operating on thin margins. Our partnership and Leadership Council with Centene seeks to address challenges CHCs experience in providing high-quality care to health center patients. CHCs need sustainable and diversified sources of funding and support in order to continue delivering services to 52 million patients, or 1 in 7 Americans, including 1 in 3 people in rural areas."

Kyu Rhee, MD, MPP, CEO and President of NACHC
