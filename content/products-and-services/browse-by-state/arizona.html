Arizona Complete Health

Arizona Complete Health has a proud history of serving communities across Arizona through Medicaid, Medicare Advantage, and Marketplace health plans.

As a leading health plan in the state, Arizona Complete Health is committed to improving the health and well-being of the people we serve. We believe healthier individuals build stronger families and thriving communities, which is why our approach is centered on whole-person care, local engagement and expanding access to quality healthcare services across Arizona.

Arizona Complete Health-Complete Care Plan is Arizona Complete Health's statewide Medicaid health plan, serving AHCCCS members across Arizona. The plan provides comprehensive medical, behavioral health and pharmacy benefits, along with care coordination and support services designed to help members achieve their best health.

Through an integrated, whole-person approach to care, the health plan connects members to quality healthcare services, wellness programs, educational resources and community-based supports. The plan also serves as the Regional Behavioral Health Authority and crisis system operator for several counties throughout Arizona, helping ensure access to critical behavioral health and crisis services for members throughout the state.

Wellcare

In Arizona, Wellcare offers a range of Medicare products, including Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug plans (MAPD), Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (DSNP), Chronic Special Needs Plans (CSNP) and standalone Prescription Drug Plans (PDP). Our Medicare Advantage plans offer affordable coverage beyond Original Medicare, including a range of benefits such as dental, hearing, and vision services; transportation and fitness services; Spendables® Cards; and other wellness programs to help our members live better, healthier lives. Wellcare's standalone PDPs help cover the cost of prescription drugs including low monthly premiums and copays; access to a broad pharmacy network; and convenient delivery of medications. Not all benefits are available in all plans, view plans in your area.

Ambetter from Arizona Complete Health

Ambetter from Arizona Complete Health offers Arizona residents continued access to comprehensive, quality health solutions at an affordable price for individual and family coverage. With focused, compassionate and coordinated care, we help individuals and families in Arizona stay covered and live healthier lives.

In addition to on-exchange marketplace plans, Ambetter Health offers off-exchange individual health insurance plans that are compatible with Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangements (ICHRA).
