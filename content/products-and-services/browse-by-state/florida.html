Wellcare

In Florida, Wellcare offers a range of Medicare products, including Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug plans (MAPD), Medicare Advantage only plans (MA only), Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (DSNP) and standalone Prescription Drug Plans (PDP). Our Medicare Advantage plans offer affordable coverage beyond Original Medicare, including a range of benefits such as dental, hearing, and vision services; transportation and fitness services; Spendables® Cards; and other wellness programs to help our members live better, healthier lives. Wellcare's standalone PDPs help cover the cost of prescription drugs including low monthly premiums and copays; access to a broad pharmacy network; and convenient delivery of medications. Not all benefits are available in all plans, view plans in your area.

Ambetter Health

Ambetter Health provides high-quality, locally-based marketplace health plans for Florida residents. Ambetter Health plans include a variety of affordable coverage and benefit options, ensuring members can choose a plan that best fits their needs and budget. With quality healthcare solutions and focused, compassionate and coordinated care, we help individuals and families in Florida stay covered and live healthier lives.

In addition to on-exchange marketplace plans, Ambetter Health offers off-exchange individual health insurance plans that are compatible with Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangements (ICHRA).
