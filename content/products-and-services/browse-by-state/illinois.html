Wellcare

In Illinois, Wellcare offers a range of Medicare products, including Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug plans (MAPD), Medicare Advantage only plans (MA only), Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (DSNP) and standalone Prescription Drug Plans (PDP). Our Medicare Advantage plans offer affordable coverage beyond Original Medicare, including a range of benefits such as dental, hearing, and vision services; transportation and fitness services; Spendables® Cards; and other wellness programs to help our members live better, healthier lives. Wellcare's standalone PDPs help cover the cost of prescription drugs including low monthly premiums and copays; access to a broad pharmacy network; and convenient delivery of medications. Not all benefits are available in all plans, view plans in your area.
