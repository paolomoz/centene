Sunflower Health Plan

Since 2013, Sunflower Health Plan has partnered with Kansas to improve the health and well-being of people enrolled in KanCare, the state’s Medicaid program. Sunflower connects members to a broad network of providers and supports whole-person care across medical, dental and behavioral health services.

Wellcare

In Kansas, Wellcare offers a range of Medicare products, including Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug plans (MAPD), Medicare Advantage only plans (MA only), Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (DSNP) and standalone Prescription Drug Plans (PDP). Our Medicare Advantage plans offer affordable coverage beyond Original Medicare, including a range of benefits such as dental, hearing, and vision services; transportation and fitness services; Spendables® Cards; and other wellness programs to help our members live better, healthier lives. Wellcare's standalone PDPs help cover the cost of prescription drugs including low monthly premiums and copays; access to a broad pharmacy network; and convenient delivery of medications. Not all benefits are available in all plans, view plans in your area.

Ambetter from Sunflower Health Plan

Ambetter from Sunflower Health Plan combines deep local expertise with years of healthcare experience to empower Kansas residents to take charge of their health. We offer a variety of affordable coverage options that enable our members to choose a plan that fits their needs and budget.

In addition to on-exchange marketplace plans, Ambetter Health offers off-exchange individual health insurance plans that are compatible with Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangements (ICHRA).
