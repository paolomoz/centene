Coordinated Care

Coordinated Care provides free and low-cost health insurance coverage to Medicaid, foster care and Marketplace members across Washington. Coordinated Care is the sole provider of healthcare coverage for Washington’s children and youth in foster care and adoption support and to young adult alumni of foster care. Coordinated Care believes in whole-person care and treating people with dignity to deliver better health outcomes with personalized support, mental health coverage, a benefit to increase access to fruits and vegetables, support for pregnant members and more to transform the health of the communities we serve, one person at a time.

Wellcare

In Washington, Wellcare offers a range of Medicare products, including Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug plans (MAPD), Medicare Advantage only plans (MA only), Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (DSNP) and standalone Prescription Drug Plans (PDP). Our Medicare Advantage plans offer affordable coverage beyond Original Medicare, including a range of benefits such as dental, hearing, and vision services; transportation and fitness services; Spendables® Cards; and other wellness programs to help our members live better, healthier lives. Wellcare's standalone PDPs help cover the cost of prescription drugs including low monthly premiums and copays; access to a broad pharmacy network; and convenient delivery of medications. Not all benefits are available in all plans, view plans in your area.
