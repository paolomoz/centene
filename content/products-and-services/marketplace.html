Ambetter Health Insurance Plans

Over the past decade, Ambetter Health has been committed to making quality healthcare accessible to individuals and families who may not have employer-sponsored insurance, offering flexible plan options and value-added benefits like telehealth and rewards for healthy living. Through Marketplace plans, Ambetter Health offers affordable options tailored to individuals’ needs.  

Today, Centene remains one of the few companies who have successfully navigated the Affordable Care Act Marketplace. Ambetter Health serves 3.5 million members in the individual market, focusing on delivering reliable, quality-focused healthcare benefits that adapt to the changing needs of its members and the market.

Delivered through local health plans, Ambetter Health provides comprehensive coverage, including preventive and wellness services, maternity and newborn care, pediatric services, mental health and substance misuse treatment, prescription drug coverage, and more. By partnering with local health plans, Ambetter Health insurance plans transform the health of local communities we serve, one person at a time.
