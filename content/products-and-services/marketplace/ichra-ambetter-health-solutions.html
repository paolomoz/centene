What is an ICHRA?

The Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement (ICHRA) was introduced by the federal government in 2020 as a new way for employers to provide health benefits to their employees. Instead of a traditional group health plan, ICHRAs allow businesses of all sizes to offer employees a monthly contribution to help them buy healthcare services that fit their unique needs.

This approach enables:

  • Predictable costs for employers
  • Personalized plan selection for employees from any available carrier
  • Compliance with Affordable Care Act (ACA) requirements

Why ICHRAs Matter in Today’s Market

Rising healthcare costs and limited flexibility in the one-size-fits-all group plans have created challenges for businesses and employees alike. ICHRAs offer a compelling and sustainable path forward, addressing these challenges by:  

  • Expanding access to affordable, high-quality coverage
  • Allowing employees to choose plans that fit their medical and financial needs
  • Supporting employers of all sizes with cost control and administrative efficiency

ICHRAs Deliver Flexibility for Employers, Brokers, and Employees

style
band-plum
Employers: Predictable Costs and Flexibility

Employers adopting ICHRAs gain greater control over healthcare spending by setting defined contributions while offering employees more choice. This model reduces administrative burden through benefit platforms and helps businesses of all sizes remain competitive in attracting and retaining talent.

Brokers: Driving Adoption and Growth

Brokers are essential in helping employers understand how an ICHRA could be a better solution to traditional group insurance. We understand how important brokers are for adoption of ICHRA in the employer space and are actively partnering with them to foster adoption.

Employees: Personalized Coverage and Portability

ICHRAs empower employees to select plans that fit their unique medical and financial needs, including preferred doctors and prescription coverage. Because these are individual policies, employees can keep their coverage even if they change jobs, ensuring continuity of care.
