Centene’s Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS) program is designed to help members maintain their independence and stay connected to their communities by providing the right resources and care at the right time. Hear directly from our members, partners, and team members about the impact this model has in their own words.

Kevin's Story

Kevin is a Sunflower Health Plan member who became paralyzed at 19.  With the support of his care team and the services provided through the health plan, Kevin has been able to live a full and independent life. He has since been married, had kids and grandkids, and become an author and motivational speaker.

Yvette’s Story

Yvette is a Fidelis Care member who has had multiple surgeries, but with the help of her care manager, she is able to live independently.

Cori’s Story

Faced with a range of health issues, none more devastating than early-onset dementia, Superior Health Plan member Cori and her ten-year-old son have benefited immensely from the support of care manager Rhonda.

Scott's Story

Scott, a Sunshine Health Long Term Care member, shares his experience using the Participant Direction Option (PDO) program.

Safe & Sound Critical Home Repair Program

Tammy is one of many PA Health and Wellness participants who has benefited from PHW’s Safe & Sound Critical Home Repair Program.

Centene National Disability Advisory Council (CNDAC)

One way that Centene demonstrates LTSS leadership is through innovations like the creation of the Centene National Disability Advisory Council. It is comprised of a diverse group of national leaders in disability rights policy and advocacy.
