LTSS Training

 

Driven by experience and empathy, our team offers no‑cost training and support for providers and caregivers, equipping them with the knowledge, skills and resources to better serve those in long‑term services and supports.

style
rt

 


Centene is committed to supporting those who provide care to individuals receiving long-term services and supports. Whether you are a healthcare provider, work in a long-term care setting, serve as a direct care worker in home and community-based services, or are an unpaid or partially paid caregiver, you play a vital role in keeping the individuals we serve healthy, safe and supported. Our webinars offer online learning opportunities designed to strengthen care delivery by equipping caregivers and care providers with practical knowledge and skills. The webinars are free and can be joined from anywhere.
