Centene’s NCQA Health Plan Accreditations

The National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) helps improve health care quality. NCQA evaluates health plans in meeting standards and guidelines through an accreditation process which evaluates operational policies, data used to monitor operations, care and services outcomes, implementation of quality improvement process, and verification of processes related to health care management.

Centene Corporation's health plans have several NCQA accreditations and distinctions:

  • Health Plan Accreditation (HPA) measures health plan performance related to quality improvement, population health, network management, utilization management, credentialing, and member experience.
  • Health Outcomes Accreditation (HOA)  provides a data-driven understanding of the health plan’s population and their unique needs and experiences, identifying and addressing gaps and disparities in care.
  • Community-Focused Care Accreditation (CFCA)  assists health plans to cultivate community-based partnerships to connect members to non-medical or social needs resources.
  • Long-Term Services & Supports (LTSS) Distinction provides a framework for organizations to deliver person-centered care that meets individual’s needs and state requirements.

Core Certification

Centene standardizes the information given to healthcare providers. The Committee on Operating Rules for Information Exchange (CORE) is an initiative by a nonprofit alliance of health plans, networks and trade associations to simplify healthcare administration. With CORE certification, Centene joins others in standardizing the information sent to providers, so they receive consistent, uniform information from each insurer. CORE certification helps providers offer the best possible care consistently to each member, which in turn helps members maintain better health. It also improves interactions between health insurers and providers, reduces costs and administrative hassles, meets HIPAA requirements, and increases accuracy and speed in paying claims. Centene has received the CAQH CORE® health plan certification seal and is exchanging electronic administrative data in compliance with the CORE rules. For more information about CORE, please visit CAQH.
