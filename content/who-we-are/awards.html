Centene is proud to be recognized by leading organizations for our performance, innovation, leadership, and culture that is grounded in shared values. These distinctions reflect how we serve our members and the strength of One CenTeam.

Enterprise Performance & Innovation  

 

Fortune 500® (2026)

Centene ranks No. 19 on the Fortune 500® list of the largest U.S. corporations by revenue. Debuting on the list in 2010 at No. 486, this marks Centene’s 17th year as a Fortune 500 honoree.

Fortune  Global 500® (2025)  

Centene ranks No. 42 on the Fortune Global 500® list, which ranks the top 500 companies worldwide based on revenue. This marks Centene’s tenth year on the list.

Fortune® World's Most Admired Companies™ (2026)  

Centene has been named one of the World's Most Admired Companies™ by Fortune®  for the eighth consecutive year. The rankings are determined by senior executives, directors, and analysts who rate enterprises within their own industries and evaluate them on nine criteria, including investment value, quality of management and products, social responsibility, and ability to attract talent.

Fortune® America’s Most Innovative Companies (2025)  

Centene has been named one of America’s Most Innovative Companies by Fortune for the third consecutive year. The list, based on input from over 40,000 employees and experts, recognizes leadership in product innovation, process innovation, and innovation culture.

Fortune® AIQ 50 (2025)

In Fortune’s inaugural list, Centene was identified as one of the top companies across a broad swath of industries that have made significant progress integrating artificial intelligence technology into their operations and seen real impact across their organizations.  

The Wall Street Journal’s Best Companies for the Future (2026)

Centene was recognized in The Wall Street Journal’s inaugural “Best Companies for the Future” ranking, developed with Bendable Labs, earning the No. 251 spot among S&P 500 companies evaluated on AI readiness, innovation, talent readiness, financial fitness, resilience and agility.

TIME’s America’s Best Companies for Future Leaders (2026)

Centene was named to this inaugural list as one of the top 175 companies where U.S. leaders were most likely to work on their way to the top.

USA TODAY’s America’s Climate Leaders (2026)

Centene was recognized by USA Today as one of America's Climate Leaders 2026. Centene joins 499 other companies on the list, based on our ability to reduce annualized emission intensity in prior years.

Leadership Recognition  

 

Sarah London – Modern Healthcare’s 100 Most Influential People in Healthcare (2025)

CEO Sarah London was named to Modern Healthcare’s list of the 100 Most Influential People in Healthcare for the third consecutive year. This recognition highlights her leadership at Centene and the team’s achievements in enhancing member experience, engaging clinicians, and addressing social drivers of health – such as food insecurity – through innovative programs, including the ethical use of AI tools.

Sarah London – Modern Healthcare’s Leading Women Vanguard Award (2025)

Modern Healthcare selected CEO Sarah London as the recipient of its 2025 Leading Women Vanguard Award. London has been recognized for her career achievements and how the impact of her work has blazed trails for other women.

Alice Hm Chen – Modern Healthcare’s 50 Most Influential Clinical Executives (2026)  

Dr. Chen, Centene’s Chief Health Officer, has been named to Modern Healthcare’s 50 Most Influential Clinical Executives for the fourth consecutive year. This program honors licensed clinicians in executive roles who are recognized by the senior editors of Modern Healthcare and selected for their exceptional achievements in driving innovation, improving outcomes, serving their communities, and demonstrating exemplary leadership both within and beyond their organizations.  

Sarah London – Fortune Most Powerful Women (2024)  

London ranked No. 60 on Fortune’s annual list, which recognizes top women leaders globally for their influence and achievements. This marks London’s third year on the list.

Sarah London – CNBC Changemakers: Women Transforming Business (2024)  

Centene CEO Sarah London was named to CNBC’s inaugural list recognizing women who have driven meaningful business and financial impact over the past year.

Workplace Excellence

Becker’s Healthcare – 165 Top Places to Work in Healthcare (2026)

Centene was named to this list based on nominations and editorial research recognizing healthcare organizations that are setting the standard for exceptional workplaces. Honorees are recognized for offering meaningful opportunities for professional and personal development, mission-driven work, and resources that support true work-life balance.

Forbes® America’s Best Employers for Company Culture™ (2026)

Centene was named to the 2026 Forbes® America’s Best Employers for Company Culture™ list for the second year in a row. Ranking 49 out of 600 employers, the list recognizes Centene within the top 20 of U.S. insurance companies.

Fair360 – Top Companies for Verified Meritocracy (2025)

Since 2018, Centene has earned annual recognition from Fair360 regarding our inclusive business practices. This recognition evaluates leadership accountability, human capital metrics, talent programs, workforce practices, economic impact, and philanthropy.

Disability Equality Index (2025)  

Centene received a 100% score for the eighth consecutive year and was named a Best Place to Work for Disability Inclusion. The DEI, a joint initiative of the American Association of People with Disabilities and Disability:IN, is a leading assessment tool for corporate disability inclusion.

Human Rights Campaign – Equality 100 Award (2025)  

Centene earned a score of 100% on the Corporate Equality Index (CEI) for the sixth consecutive year, receiving the Equality 100 Award: Leader in LGBTQ+ Workplace Inclusion. The CEI evaluates policies and practices around LGBTQ+ workplace equality.  

National Organization on Disability – Leading Disability Employer (2025)  

Centene was named a Leading Disability Employer for the fifth consecutive year. This award recognizes companies with outstanding hiring and employment practices for people with disabilities.  

Fortune Best Workplaces in Health Care™ (2025)  

Centene ranked No. 16 in the Large Company category. Evaluated by Great Place to Work®, the award measures employee experience across demographic groups and roles. This marks Centene’s fourth consecutive year on the list.

Newsweek– America's Best Workplaces for Women™ (2026) 

Centene has been named to Newsweek’s America's Best Workplaces for Women™ list. This ranking recognizes standout companies actively dismantling gender barriers through equitable policies and workplace culture while fostering environments in which women thrive.  

Newsweek – America's Greatest Workplaces for Culture, Belonging, and Community (2026)

For the fourth consecutive year, Centene was awarded this recognition which honors the greatest workplaces in the United States that foster welcoming work environments.  

Newsweek – America's Most Admired Workplaces (2026)

Centene was named to America’s Most Admired Workplaces 2026, which highlights 750 U.S. companies that are leading examples of best workplace practices, creating environments that are admired by employees.  

Newsweek – America’s Greatest Workplaces for Gen Z (2025) 

For the second year in a row, Centene received a five-star rating among 1,000 U.S. Companies excelling in areas that matter most to young professionals: belonging and community, sustainability and awareness, compensation and benefits, training and career progression, work-life balance, working environment, company image, mental wellbeing, job security, and corporate culture.   

Newsweek – America's Greatest Workplaces Parents & Families (2025)  

For the third year in a row, Centene was named to Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces for Parents & Families, which recognizes the greatest workplaces in the U.S. by their performance on a range of relevant metrics from the perspective of parents.  

Age Friendly Institute – Certified Age Friendly Employer (2025-2026)

For the first time in 2025, Centene completed the Age Friendly Institute’s certification for Certified Age Friendly Employers. This two-year certification is reflective of Centene’s commitment to providing inclusive opportunities, community and support for employees of all ages and generations.

TIME/Statista – World’s Best Companies (2025)  

Centene was named to TIME’sWorld’s Best Companies 2025 ranking, which identifies the top-performing companies across the globe. The companies were identified based on three primary dimensions: employee satisfaction, revenue growth, and sustainability (ESG).  

Great Places to Work® (2025)  

For the fifth consecutive year, Centene has been recertified as a Great Place to Work, a premier workplace certification recognizing the company as an employer of choice.  

U.S. News & World Report – Best Companies to Work For (2024–2025)  

Centene was named one of the Best Companies to Work For in the health services industry. Rankings are based on six factors: pay and benefits, work/life balance, stability, comfort, belonging, and career development.  

RippleMatch – Campus Forward Award (2025)  

Centene received the Campus Forward Award in the Large Early Career Program category. This recognition highlights companies with outstanding student-nominated internship and early career recruitment programs, focused on candidate experience and impact.  

U.S. Veterans Magazine – Top Veteran-Friendly Company (2025)  

Centene was recognized for the sixth consecutive year and named for having a Top Supplier Diversity Program for Veterans.  

Military Times – Best for Vets Employers (2025)  

Centene has been recognized on this list since 2020 for providing recruitment, leadership programs, benefits, and support for veterans and their families.
