Employee Networks at Centene

style
rt

Our Employee Networks, which include our Employee Inclusion Groups (EIGs) and our Centene Professional Networks (CPNs), promote our One CenTeam culture by making space for innovation, collaboration and networking. These volunteer- led groups are open to all employees, and offer professional and leadership development programs, contribute to community impact initiatives, and support business innovation and corporate best practices.

rt
People with Disabilities &amp; Caregivers

The mission of the People with Disabilities & Caregivers Employee Inclusion Group, ABILITY, is to support and enable employees to fully contribute and reach their full potential in the workplace. The EIG works to challenge the stereotypes and stigma associated with people with disabilities and to strengthen understanding and awareness of disability across the company.

style
rt
Veterans and Military Families

Our commitment to engagement with veterans and military families extends to our workforce. Centene's Veterans and Military Families Employee Inclusion Group, CENVET, was established to provide support and resources for employees who have served in the armed forces and for those related to a service member or former service member.

style

LGBTQIA+

cPRIDE, Centene's LGBTQIA+ Employee Inclusion Group, supports an inclusive work environment for lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, intersex, and all employees within the spectrum of gender and sexuality. The EIG works to provide development, support, and networking opportunities for its members while providing guidance on how Centene can be an even greater place for LGBTQIA+ employees to work.

Women

I.N.S.P.I.R.E: Influence. Network. Support. Promote. Include. Recognize. Empower.

INSPIRE, Centene's Women and Allies Employee Inclusion Group helps members meet their professional and personal goals at all career and life stages. The EIG leverages mentoring, networking and community engagement to keep Centene as an employer of choice among professional women.

style
rt
Multicultural

MOSAIC, our Multicultural Employee Inclusion Group provides peer support for employees of all racial and ethnic backgrounds through networking, mentoring and coaching. It also promotes education and awareness and supports employee engagement and retention.

style
rt
Intergenerational

STAGES: Sharing. Togetherness. Adaptability. Growth. Empathy. Strength.

STAGES, Centene’s intergenerational Employee Inclusion Group, works to build connections and community between the five generations who make up Centene's workforce. This intergenerational network helps to deepen our sense of belonging and promote diversity of thought, innovation and knowledge-sharing to better connect us with the members we serve.
