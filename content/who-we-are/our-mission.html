Our Mission

Transforming the health of the communities we serve, one person at a time.
Since our founding as a single local healthcare plan in 1984, Centene’s mission has been focused on helping people live healthier lives. From that day until now, our company has worked tirelessly to increase access to affordable, quality-focused healthcare to those who need it most.
This long-held commitment to supporting low-income and vulnerable populations for which access to care has historically been a challenge is captured in our mission to transform the health of the communities we serve, one person at a time.
