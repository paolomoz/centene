Health Plans: Serving Members and Their Communities

Caring for our members, and the communities they call home, means being where they are. Centene’s unwavering commitment to a uniquely local approach is demonstrated every day by its state-based health plans. Our experience in healthcare began as a single Medicaid health plan in Wisconsin that now stretches from coast to coast and beyond. We provide fully integrated, affordable and quality-focused services to government-sponsored and commercial healthcare programs, focusing on underserved populations.

Even as we expand, we are able to remain attentive to the unique needs of the communities we serve by building a local presence in each of our markets from the ground up. Working hand in hand with local healthcare providers and other groups that share our dedication, our employees help members access care, coordinate referrals to health and social services, and address concerns and questions.

Specialty Services: Placing People First

Effectively serving a diverse range of individuals means caring for each person’s unique needs. With our portfolio of specialty services, Centene is able to offer a full continuum of health solutions that help bring to life our commitment to whole health and a focus on the individual.

Centene's Health Care Enterprise group addresses the unique challenges among the individuals we serve. With capabilities ranging from data analytics to case management solutions, in-home primary care, and intellectual/developmental disability support services and therapies, these innovative companies help provide the appropriate insights and resources to empower more people to take control of their health.
