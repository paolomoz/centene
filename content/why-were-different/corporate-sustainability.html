Corporate Sustainability

 

Centene’s Commitment

As a leading healthcare enterprise serving nearly 26 million members, Centene has a responsibility to deliver meaningful impact for our members when they need it most and to create innovative programs that address barriers to care. Our approach integrates principles of corporate responsibility and strong governance across all facets of our operations. Together, we are driving a sustainable enterprise focused on building healthy and resilient communities, establishing Centene as a preferred partner for our state and federal government customers, and empowering our employees to do their best work while delivering value for our stakeholders. Together, we are shaping a future where healthcare is provided as a service to build a healthier future for all.

 

Corporate Sustainability Framework

Centene’s Corporate Sustainability Framework reflects how we deliver on our mission — transforming the health of the communities we serve, one person at a time — and it expresses Centene’s commitments to empowering health, building healthier communities, fostering a healthy environment and driving business accountability while identifying focus areas aligned with our strategy and essential to our mission.

Empowering Health
 

Provide affordable, quality-focused healthcare that helps people live healthier lives.

  • Healthcare Quality
  • Healthcare Access and Drivers of Health
  • Healthcare Innovation and Thought Leadership
  • Customer Experience and Relationship Management

Building Healthier Communities
 

Enhance the lives of the communities we serve and the people who make our business successful.

  • Culture, Talent and Well-being
  • One CenTeam
  • Community Impact and Giving

Fostering a Healthy Environment
 

Build resilience to environment-related health risks and support earth-friendly initiatives for a livable future.

  • Environmental Impacts on Health
  • Environmental Sustainability

Driving Business Accountability


Foster a culture of accountability and maintain the trust of our members.

  • Governance and Accountability
  • Ethics and Compliance
  • Data Privacy and Security
  • Risk Management
  • Public Policy
