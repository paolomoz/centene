At Centene, we are dedicated to evaluating climate and other environmental risks, understanding their impact on the health of our members, and managing our operations with environmental awareness. Environmental impacts on health are often disproportionately experienced by the communities we serve, and our aim is to enhance our members’ resilience through thoughtful local solutions.
 


Environmental Impacts on Health

We recognize that the environment in which people live has a major impact on their health, and that climate change is bringing new challenges to the communities we serve. By working together, Centene partnerships help remove barriers to health and address health-related social needs like heating and air conditioning, shelter, and food security that could be impacted by environmental factors.
