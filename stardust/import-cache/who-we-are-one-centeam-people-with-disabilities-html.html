An Enabled Workforce
 

According to the United States Department of Labor’s Office of Disability Employment Policy, people with disabilities make up the third-largest market segment in the United States. By ensuring equal access to employment opportunities for everyone, including individuals with disabilities, our workforce encompasses a broad range of lived experiences that help us address our members’ needs. Just as we are committed to hiring the most qualified talent, Centene is equally committed to ensuring that every member of our workforce has access to the tools and resources that enable them to be successful at work.

Centene National Disability Advisory Council

To help provide a supportive environment for people with disabilities, Centene worked with key leaders in the disability community to establish a council to develop a framework for disability inclusion with respect to our employees, members, providers, and suppliers. Our National Disability Advisory Council includes leaders in disability advocacy who develop recommendations and propose initiatives that advance disability inclusion in Centene's workforce and product offerings in our healthcare solutions. 

 

"I am grateful for the opportunity to lead an independent council that includes world-class leaders across the disability community and to partner with Centene to help them be the best healthcare company for our community as an insurer, an employer and a business partner."

- Andy Imparato, CNDAC Chair, Disability Rights California

National Employer Recognition

As a result of our approach to inclusion of people with disabilities in our workplace, we have been named one of the best places to work for people with disabilities by the Disability Equality Index (DEI®) for six consecutive years.

The Disability Equality Index is a joint initiative by Disability:IN and the American Association of People with Disabilities (AAPD). Developed by the group’s advisory committee, a diverse collaborative of business leaders, policy experts, and disability advocates, the Index is a national, transparent, annual benchmarking tool that offers businesses an opportunity to receive an objective score on their disability inclusion policies and practices.
