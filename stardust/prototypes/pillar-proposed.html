        State & National Health Plan Solutions

        EVERY COMMUNITY, AND EVERY PERSON, IS UNIQUE, AND OUR BREADTH OF OFFERINGS GIVES US THE REACH WE NEED TO BRING OUR MEMBERS AFFORDABLE, QUALITY-FOCUSED HEALTHCARE WITH DIGNITY.

        Medicaid

        We partner with state governments to provide health coverage to millions of eligible low-income adults, children and pregnant women.

        Medicaid

        Medicare

        Our Medicare Advantage and Medicare-Medicaid plans provide access to personal, local care, and promote health and independence.

        Medicare

        Health Insurance Marketplace

        We provide affordable, comprehensive plans for individuals and families who may not qualify for Medicaid or other government coverage.

        Health Insurance Marketplace

        Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangements (ICHRAs)

        Centene, through Ambetter Health Solutions, is driving the evolution of employer-sponsored health coverage with health plans designed for Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangements (ICHRAs)

        Learn More >

        Long-Term Services & Supports (LTSS)

        Centene is a national leader in managed long-term services and supports (LTSS). By delivering person-centered care through deep expertise, a local approach, and innovative solutions that improve outcomes, Centene helps members maintain their independence.

        Learn More >

        Foster Care

        Centene serves more children involved in the child welfare and foster care system than any Medicaid managed care organization in the nation. We help members and their caregivers get the right care at the right time.
